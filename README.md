# Surah Al-Waqiah
Surah Al-Waqiah (The Inevitable Event) is the 56th surah of the Quran, with 96 verses revealed in Makkah.
- Surah number: 56 of 114
- Verses: 96
- Revelation place: Makkah
- Ruku: 3
- Juz: 27
- Theme: The certainty of the Day of Judgment
## About this surah
Surah Al-Waqiah describes the Day of Judgment and divides mankind into three groups: the foremost, the companions of the right, and the companions of the left. It points to Allah's power in creation — crops, water and fire — and ends with the moment of death and a command to glorify Allah.
